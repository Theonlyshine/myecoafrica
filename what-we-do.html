What We Do

Three arms.
One engine.

Research & Intelligence. Media & Communications. Education. Three distinct disciplines that feed into each other — not three separate services that happen to share a name.

01
Research & Intelligence

Data that decisions can actually be built on.

We produce commissioned research, policy analysis, and sustainability intelligence reports for organisations, development finance institutions, NGOs, and government bodies. Our work is built on synthesis and original analysis — not aggregated press releases and not the kind of "research" that starts with the conclusion.

We work with organisations that need to understand a specific question about Africa's sustainability landscape: what is happening, why it is happening, and what should happen next.

Media & Communications

The research means nothing if the right people never hear it.

We produce documentary films, branded content campaigns, editorial work, and communications strategies that make organisations, funders, and the public understand what the research actually means — and act on it.

We work with organisations that have something important to say about sustainability in Africa and need it communicated in a way that actually lands. Not a glossy corporate video. Work that earns attention because it is honest about the complexity.

03
Education

The generation that will run Africa's sustainability future is in secondary school right now.

GESLP — the Green Enterprise Sustainability Leadership Program — is Africa's first structured sustainability leadership program designed for secondary school students. It is fully facilitated by MyEcoAfrica, free of charge, with zero burden on school staff.

We handle delivery, documentation, photography, video, and the end-of-programme showcase. Schools and sponsors get the proof. Students get the certification, the skills, and the platform.

What We Do

Three arms. One engine.

We work with schools, development institutions, NGOs, energy companies, and government agencies. Everything we do points at one goal: a continent of people who are sustainability-literate and know what to do with that knowledge.

Research & Intelligence

We produce commissioned research, policy analysis, and sustainability governance advisory built on rigorous synthesis of existing data and literature, sharpened by original analysis.

  • Commissioned research for NGOs, think tanks, and institutions
  • Governance and policy analysis
  • White papers for development finance institutions
  • Advisory retainers for strategic transition work
  • Conference speaking and expert commentary

Media & Communications

Nobody changes policy, funding, or public opinion because of a PDF. They change it because someone made them feel the cost of not acting.

We produce documentary, branded content, and editorial work for energy companies, NGOs, and development institutions who need Africa's sustainability transition told by people who actually understand it, not a production crew parachuted in for b-roll.

  • Documentary and impact film production
  • Branded content campaigns
  • Editorial content and policy briefs
  • Op-eds, newsletters, stakeholder communications
  • Impact documentation for foundations

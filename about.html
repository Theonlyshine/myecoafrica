About

We research it.
We tell it.
We teach it.

MyEcoAfrica is West Africa's sustainability intelligence company. We exist because the organisations, institutions, and people working to close Africa's sustainability gap need better research, better storytelling, and a next generation that is actually equipped to lead.

The Organisation

Built from the inside. Not observing from the outside.

MyEcoAfrica is not a consulting firm that happened to pick Africa as a market. We are embedded in Africa's sustainability transition. The research comes from people who understand the terrain. The media is made for the audiences who actually live the story. The education reaches the students who will run the institutions next.

We operate as three arms — Research & Intelligence, Media & Communications, and Education — that feed into each other. Research informs what media we make. Media amplifies what research finds. Education equips the people who will generate and act on the research of the next generation.

Why We Exist

The institutions shaping Africa's sustainability future need intelligence that is actually accurate.

"Africa's sustainability transition is real and it is happening. But the data being used to make decisions about it is often incomplete, the stories being told about it are often inaccurate, and the people who will run it next are often unprepared."

MyEcoAfrica exists to change that. Not through advocacy, but through the unglamorous work of actually building institutional knowledge: commissioning rigorous research, producing media that is honest about the complexity, and training the next generation to understand what they are inheriting.

Research Team

Head of Media

Media & Communications Team

Our media arm produces documentary content, editorial publishing, and strategic communications for institutions building in Africa's sustainability transition. The team operates on the conviction that the story is part of the work — not decoration on top of it.

GESLP Programme Team

The GESLP team designs, deploys, and monitors the Green Eco Schools Leadership Program across Nigeria and expanding West Africa markets. In Year One the team delivered workshops across 7 states with 700+ students trained, all at zero cost to participating schools.

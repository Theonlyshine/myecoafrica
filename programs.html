1 million Africans equipped
through GESLP by 2030.

GESLP is the education arm of MyEcoAfrica. It is free for every school and every student.

1M
The Target

One million Africans with the knowledge to lead Africa's sustainability transition.

GESLP — the Global Energy and Sustainability Leadership Program — is Africa's first structured sustainability leadership program designed for secondary school students. Fully facilitated by MyEcoAfrica. Completely free. Zero burden on school staff.

Currently Running

Two-Day Climate Workshop

The active programme under GESLP. Delivered in secondary schools across West Africa. Students get hands-on sustainability literacy, leadership development, and a certificate of participation. Professional photography and video documentation of every cohort. Free for every school and every student.

What Students Get

Not a workshop. A launchpad.

01

Academic Value

Global Sustainability Literacy Certificate and research training. Verifiable proof that means something to universities and future employers.

02

Leadership

Communication, public speaking, and mentoring. The kind of confidence that is visible and measurable — not just on a CV.

03

Real Projects

A genuine research project and personal portfolio. Not a worksheet — actual work they can show to the world and build on.

04

Visibility

Professional photography, video, and features across MyEcoAfrica platforms. The evidence is durable and follows them.

The Target

One million Africans equipped to lead Africa's sustainability transition.

GESLP is Africa's first structured sustainability leadership program for secondary schools. Free for every school and every student. Zero burden on school staff.

What Students Get

Two days. Two tracks. One certificate.

01

Track 1 — Solutions

Students analyse a real sustainability challenge in their community, identify what already works, and present a structured response.

02

Track 2 — Advocacy

Students build and deliver a case for change. Public speaking, evidence-based argument, direct engagement with a live audience.

03

Roundtable Sessions

Structured peer and expert discussions. Students argue, defend positions, and refine their thinking with facilitators in the room.

04

Student Q&A

Direct engagement with sustainability practitioners and subject experts. Real questions, real answers, on the record.

05

Sustainability Literacy Certificate

Every student who completes the programme receives a certificate verifiable by schools, universities, and employers.

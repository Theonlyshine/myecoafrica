GESLP

Live in secondary schools, because that's where the access already exists. Professional documentation of every cohort. Free for every school and every student.

Currently Running

Two-day Climate Workshop

The current active programme under GESLP. Delivered in secondary schools across West Africa, the two-day climate workshop gives students hands-on sustainability literacy, leadership development, and a certificate of participation. Professional photography and video documentation of every cohort. Free for every school and every student.

Pillar 01

Academic Value

Global Sustainability Literacy Certificate and research training. Verifiable proof that means something to universities.

Pillar 02

Leadership

Communication, public speaking, and mentoring. The kind of confidence that is visible and measurable.

Pillar 03

Real Projects

A genuine research project and personal portfolio. Not a worksheet, actual work they can show to the world.

Pillar 04

Visibility

Professional photography, video, and features across MyEcoAfrica platforms. The evidence is durable.

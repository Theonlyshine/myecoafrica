The Global Energy and Sustainability Leadership Program

Every conference held with a speaker over fifty is one more room full of people who'll be retired before Africa's sustainability transition is done. The people who'll actually run it next don't have a platform yet.

GESLP puts sustainability literacy directly into secondary schools, free for every participating school and student, with professional documentation of every cohort and a showcase event at the end of each run.

West Africa's sustainability institutions are more ambitious on paper than they are equipped in practice. Across the region, national climate commitments are being made at the policy level without the institutional infrastructure to implement, measure, or report on them credibly.

This brief examines the gap between stated policy objectives and institutional capacity across five key West African economies: Nigeria, Ghana, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, and Benin. It draws on a combination of desk research, institutional mapping, and primary interviews conducted between October and December 2025.

The findings are not surprising to those who work inside these institutions — but they matter because the gap is being systematically underreported in the data that international funders, development finance institutions, and policy advocates are using to make decisions.

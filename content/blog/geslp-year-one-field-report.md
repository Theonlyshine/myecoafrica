The Global Energy and Sustainability Leadership Program launched its first full cohort year in 2025. This report documents what we learned — from schools, from students, from the administrative staff who had to integrate a new curriculum without additional burden, and from the data we collected on learning outcomes and sustainability literacy across the cohort.

The core question GESLP was designed to answer is whether sustainability education at secondary level is structurally achievable in contexts where school resources are constrained, teacher bandwidth is limited, and curricular flexibility is narrow. The year one data gives us a provisional answer: it is achievable if the design assumption is zero additional burden on school staff.

This report covers deployment across four states in West Africa, documents the operational model that allowed free delivery at scale, and presents a preliminary analysis of student outcome data from pre- and post-cohort assessments.

It is written for educational institutions considering adoption, development finance partners assessing impact metrics, and corporate or governmental entities evaluating sponsorship or co-deployment at scale.
